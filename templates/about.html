About StudyMateAI

StudyMateAI is an AI-powered learning assistant designed to help students understand concepts, solve homework, generate quizzes, explain programming topics, and enhance learning through intelligent conversations. Built using Flask and the Groq API, StudyMateAI delivers accurate, fast, and easy-to-understand responses for students across different subjects.

What Makes StudyMateAI Special?

AI Powered

Uses the Groq API to generate intelligent, real-time responses for academic queries.

Easy Learning

Simplifies difficult concepts with step-by-step explanations and examples.

Programming Help

Assists students with coding, debugging and explanation of programs.

Fast Responses

Powered by Groq to provide lightning-fast AI answers.
